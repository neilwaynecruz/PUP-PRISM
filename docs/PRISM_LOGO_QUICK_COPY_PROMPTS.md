# PUP PRISM Quick Copy Logo Prompts

Use these when you want shorter prompts for tools with less prompt space.

---

## 1. Best Short Prompt

```text
Create a premium modern logo for "PUP PRISM", an enterprise inventory, asset tracking, stock issuance, booking, and handover management system. The logo should communicate clarity, structure, control, trust, and operational intelligence. Use a clean geometric style with a strong icon that can work as an app tile or sidebar icon, plus a professional wordmark. Favor a refined blue and slate palette that looks excellent in both light mode and dark mode. Avoid playful, mascot-like, rainbow-prism, or generic academic seal styles. Make it minimal, memorable, scalable, and production-ready.
```

---

## 2. Icon-Only Prompt

```text
Design an icon-only logo for "PUP PRISM", a professional inventory and asset operations platform. The icon should feel geometric, intelligent, clean, and authoritative. It should suggest clarity, structure, inventory control, visibility, and operational precision. Use a blue-forward palette that works on both light and dark backgrounds. The mark must be highly recognizable at small sizes and suitable for a sidebar icon, app tile, and favicon.
```

---

## 3. Full Logo Prompt

```text
Design a full brand logo for "PUP PRISM" with both symbol and wordmark. PUP PRISM is a property, resource, inventory, stock movement, booking, and handover management system. The design should feel modern, reliable, institutional, and digitally advanced. Create a symbol that can also stand alone as an app icon. Use a clean blue and slate palette, ensure strong contrast in light and dark themes, and keep the design minimal, balanced, and enterprise-grade.
```

---

## 4. Premium Presentation Prompt

```text
Create a polished logo presentation for "PUP PRISM" as if it were the official brand identity of a real institutional inventory and asset management platform. Show a strong primary logo, an icon-only version, light background and dark background applications, and a clean professional brand style. The logo should feel structured, trustworthy, intelligent, and memorable, with subtle visual references to inventory, organization, tracking, or prism-like clarity.
```

---

## 5. Refinement Prompt

```text
Keep the current concept, but refine it to feel more iconic, more balanced, and more aligned with enterprise inventory software. Improve the spacing, silhouette, geometry, and small-size legibility. Make the icon stronger for sidebar and app-icon use. Preserve the blue-centered professional palette and ensure the logo works perfectly on both light and dark backgrounds.
```

---

## 6. Negative Prompt / Direction Filter

Use this together with any generator that accepts negative prompts or exclusions:

```text
Avoid cartoon style, mascot logos, rainbow prism effects, generic shields, overly academic emblems, excessive gradients, cluttered linework, thin fragile details, soft startup aesthetics, gaming vibes, crypto branding, and anything that feels generic or templated.
```
